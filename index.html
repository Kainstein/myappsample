What is a Spinning Drop Tensometer?

The Spinning Drop Tensometer is a sophisticated analytical instrument designed to measure ultra-low interfacial tension between two immiscible liquids. Based on the spinning drop method, this technique is particularly valuable when conventional methods reach their detection limits.

Operating Principle

The instrument operates on a fundamental principle:

  1. A droplet of less dense liquid is placed in a rotating tube filled with a denser liquid
  2. As the tube rotates at high speed, centrifugal forces elongate the droplet
  3. The droplet shape reaches equilibrium between centrifugal force and interfacial tension
  4. By measuring the droplet dimensions and rotation speed, interfacial tension is calculated using the Vonnegut equation

Vonnegut Equation:

γ = ΔρR³ω² / 4

where γ is interfacial tension, Δρ is density difference, R is droplet radius, and ω is angular velocity
